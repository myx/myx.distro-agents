# Working with The Conclave

The Conclave is a crew of specialised AI teammates. They cover architecture, DevOps, frontend, documentation, testing and more, and `magic-coordinator` leads them. Its name is Magic.

# Asking for something

- Start a message with `Magic,` to reach the coordinator. It decides who takes the work.
- Name a member instead, such as `magic-architect`, when you already know who owns the work.
- Ask for one thing per message, and put the ask first.
- Expect a short readback of what was understood before work starts.

# Where the conversation lives

- Each piece of work gets its own thread in the team's shared chat channel.
- Updates, questions and the closing summary all go into that thread.
- A question that needs your decision comes to you directly, as a message you can answer with a reply.
- Reply in the thread you were asked in, so the answer reaches the right session.

# Where the work is tracked

- Every task, question and proposal is an item on the team board.
- Items move through states from backlog to pending, running and processed.
- The coordinator keeps the board. Ask `Magic,` for the state of anything, rather than looking for the files.

# What the team will not do

- It does not commit, push or deploy for you. Changes wait in your working tree for your review.
- It does not act as you, or speak in your name.
